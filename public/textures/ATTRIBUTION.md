# Earth surface texture

`earth-blue-marble-july.jpg` is the unmodified NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation July 2004 map with topography and bathymetry shading (5400 × 2700).

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory. Produced by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.

Source and usage credit: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/
Map download page: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/
Original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg

This historical monthly composite is a visual background, not real-time imagery or a surveyed classroom map. Administrative boundaries and classroom annotations are separate layers. No Google imagery is distributed in this project.
